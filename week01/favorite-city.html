My Favorite City

The name of my favorite city is Ibadan

It is the largest city in Africa

It has a metropolitan population of over 4.2 million as at the last censor.

Ibadan is famous for its iconic, traditional brown zinc roofs that stretch across its older landscape.

One of the famous landmark in Ibadan is the National Museum of Unity, this museum features four main galleries-Unity, Yoruba, Masquerade, and Pottery.
